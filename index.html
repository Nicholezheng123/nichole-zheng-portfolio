About Me

hi there...

I'm Nichole, a junior majoring in Operations Research and Information Engineering with a minor in Data Science at Cornell University. I enjoy turning messy datasets into clear insights and building solutions that improve decision-making, efficiency, and impact.

My projects draw on a strong foundation in data analytics, statistics, and optimization. I've built end-to-end analyses using Python and SQL, developed regression and machine learning models, and created visualizations to support data-driven decisions. These projects include applied coursework, research, and independent work, where I've tackled problems involving efficiency, performance evaluation, and real-world constraints.

Outside the classroom, I'm involved in teaching roles that reflect my values around clear communication, teamwork, and helping others learn. As a Teaching Assistant for CS 2850 and an Academic Excellence Workshop Facilitator, I work with students to break down challenging technical topics in ways that are easy to understand. These experiences have shaped how I explain ideas clearly, work with different audiences, and support learning in a practical, approachable way.
